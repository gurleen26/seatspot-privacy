# SeatSpot – Account and Data Deletion ## How to request account deletion SeatSpot users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by emailing: **gurleenkaur2k5@gmail.com** Please use the subject line **"SeatSpot Account Deletion Request"** and include the email address registered with your SeatSpot account. ## What data will be deleted? After verifying your request, we will delete: - Your Firebase Authentication account. - Your associated user profile and personal information stored in Firestore. - Your associated seat reservation and booking records stored in Firestore. ## Data retention We aim to process verified deletion requests within **30 days**. Once processed, the account and associated data listed above will be deleted from our active Firebase Authentication and Firestore records. Any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons will be retained only for the period required. ## Contact us For questions about account deletion or data privacy, contact: **Email:** gurleenkaur2k5@gmail.com **App:** SeatSpot
