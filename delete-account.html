SeatSpot – Account and Data Deletion

How to request account deletion

SeatSpot users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by emailing:

gurleenkaur2k5@gmail.com

Use the subject line SeatSpot Account Deletion Request and include the email address registered with your SeatSpot account.

What data will be deleted?

After verifying your request, we will delete:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your associated user profile and personal information stored in Firestore.
  • Your associated seat reservation and booking records stored in Firestore.

Data retention

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days.

The account and associated data listed above will be deleted from active Firebase Authentication and Firestore records. Any information required to be retained for legal or security reasons will be retained only for the period required.
