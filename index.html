SeatSpot Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SeatSpot ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SeatSpot mobile application.

SeatSpot is a library seat reservation application that helps users explore library facilities, check seat availability, make reservations, and use AI-assisted booking features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SeatSpot may process the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, Firebase user ID, and available profile information used during registration and login.
  • Google Sign-In information: Basic account information made available through Google Sign-In, such as your email address and name.
  • Reservation information: Seat selections, reservation dates, booking status, and related library booking information.
  • AI conversation information: Messages and requests you send to the AI booking assistant to receive recommendations or booking assistance.
  • Technical information: Limited technical information may be processed by the services used to operate and maintain the application.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information processed by SeatSpot is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Display library and seat availability information.
  • Create, manage, and update seat reservations.
  • Provide AI-assisted booking and conversational responses.
  • Maintain application functionality and troubleshoot issues.
  • Respond to user support and privacy requests.

3. Third-Party Services

SeatSpot uses third-party services to provide its functionality. These services may process information as necessary to perform their respective functions.

  • Firebase Authentication: Used for account registration and authentication.
  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore: Used to store and manage application information, including account-related and reservation data.
  • Render: Used to host the application backend and process supported application requests.
  • Google Gemini: Used to process requests submitted to the AI booking assistant and generate conversational responses.

Information shared with these providers is limited to what is necessary for the relevant application functionality. Their processing is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

4. AI Assistant and Conversation Data

SeatSpot includes an AI-assisted booking feature. When you use this feature, the messages you submit may be processed by our backend and sent to Google Gemini to generate responses.

Recent conversation messages may be used to maintain conversational context and provide relevant responses. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or other highly sensitive personal information in AI messages.

5. Data Storage and Security

SeatSpot uses Firebase services and its hosted backend infrastructure to support application functionality.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described in this policy to operate SeatSpot, provide authentication, manage reservations, and generate AI responses.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security and rights of users and the application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide application functionality, maintain user accounts, fulfill legitimate operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.
